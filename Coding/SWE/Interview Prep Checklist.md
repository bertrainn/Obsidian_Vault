## 🏆 Final Preparation

### Day Before Interview
- [ ] Review key concepts
- [ ] Practice 2-3 easy problems
- [ ] Prepare questions to ask
- [ ] Set up technical environment
- [ ] Get good night's sleep

### Day of Interview
- [ ] Arrive early (or test video setup)
- [ ] Bring notebook and pen
- [ ] Have water available
- [ ] Review company info
- [ ] Stay calm and confident

## 📝 Questions to Ask

### Technical Questions
- What's the tech stack for this role?
- How do you handle technical debt?
- What's the deployment process like?
- How do you ensure code quality?

### Team/Culture Questions
- What's the team structure?
- How do you handle work-life balance?
- What growth opportunities exist?
- How do you support learning?

### Role-Specific Questions
- What would a typical day look like?
- What are the main challenges?
- How is success measured?
- What projects would I work on?
